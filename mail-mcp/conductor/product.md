# Product Definition - mail-mcp

## Vision
A provider-agnostic email bridge for AI Agents, enabling secure and reliable email interactions through a unified MCP interface.

## Goals
- Provide a robust IMAP/SMTP implementation in pure Go.
- Support modern authentication (OAuth2 and App Passwords).
- Enable complex email workflows (searching, threading, attachments).

## Target Users
- AI Agents needing email access.
- Developers building personal assistant tools.
- Systems requiring automated email processing via LLMs.
